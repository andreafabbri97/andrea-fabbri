Custom business software

Custom business software, built around how you work

If your business runs on spreadsheets, notebooks, WhatsApp chats and a program nobody likes, I build the software around the way you actually work: your words, your steps, your rules. One system, used every day from the computer and the phone.

Write to meWhatsAppAll work

The problem

  • The same data is typed two or three times: in the spreadsheet, in the program, in the message to a colleague.
  • The software you have is made for everyone, so for nobody: half the fields are useless and what you need is missing.
  • The figures you decide on (margins, hours, costs) arrive at the end of the month, if at all, and someone rebuilds them by hand.
  • The rules of the business live in the heads of one or two people: when they are away, everything stops.

What the software does

  • One place for customers, orders, hours, stock and invoices, with nothing copied twice.
  • The rules of your trade written into the software and checked by automated tests, from a few hundred to a few thousand per project.
  • It works on the computer and on the phone, installs like an app and, where needed, works offline.
  • Roles and permissions: everyone sees what they need, and the financial figures stay private.
  • The figures you decide on are calculated as you work, not rebuilt at the end of the month.
  • Local rules handled for what they are: in San Marino a single-stage tax instead of VAT and a data-protection law of its own, not patched onto an Italian product.

How I work

  1. 1

    Understand how you work

    Before writing any code I study your work: who does what, in which order, with which exceptions. The words in the software will be yours.

  2. 2

Frequently asked questions

How much does custom software cost?+

It depends on how much it has to do and how many people will use it. Before starting I study your work and propose where to begin: often the best start is a first version that solves the most expensive problem, then growing it.

Off-the-shelf or custom?+

If an off-the-shelf product already does what you need, that is the better choice, and I will tell you. Custom makes sense when the way you work is your advantage, or when ready-made products force you to work worse.

Do you only work in San Marino?+

No: I live in San Marino but work remotely with businesses anywhere, in English and Italian, and I am happy to travel when the work is best seen in person.
